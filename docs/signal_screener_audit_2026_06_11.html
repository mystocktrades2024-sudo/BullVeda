The screener is a 6-stage funnel. ~1,800 candidates in → a handful of BUYs out.

2 · What's good, what's bad

✓ What's working

  • Architecture is institutional-grade — multi-source universe, regime conditioning, Wilson-gated kills, full audit trail per ticker.
  • Real edge exists in pockets: trending regime PF 1.59, bull 1.65; PULLBACK entries PF 1.33 (n=147); 10-Week Pullback +11.5% alpha.
  • Trend Continuation family is profitable on R-basis (PF 1.41, n=577).
  • Goes flat correctly on macro days & in stress — 0-BUY days are only CPI/NFP/FOMC days.
  • Abstention is visible, not silent — every sleeve writes a `*_audit` reason explaining why it didn't fire.
  • The system self-diagnoses honestly — strategy_validation_report.html already documents the negative alpha.

✗ What's broken

  • BUY alpha is significantly negative — −0.55%/trade (t=−2.33, p=0.020). Worse than its own β=+1.65 factor exposure.
  • Verdict isn't predictive — BUY (PF 1.00) underperforms WATCH (PF 1.26). The label adds no edge.
  • Edge is decaying: April PF 2.23 → May 1.29 → June 0.16. The "80% WR" lore is an April-only artifact.
  • Lives where it has no edge: 79% of signals fire in choppy (PF 0.99); edge only shows in trending.
  • Biggest family loses money: Breakout Expansion PF 0.90 (BUY-only 0.74), n=628.
  • entry_quality is inverted: FRESH (textbook best) PF 0.67 worst; MISSED outperforms it. Gate still demotes the profitable entries.
  • Real paper trading lost money: 62 closed, PF 0.69, −$1,344 realized. The +7.5% equity is unrealized, concentrated in one NEM short.

5 · Why it's failing — root cause

Five reinforcing mechanisms, in order of impact:

① Composition: the BUY pool is ~75% zero-alpha momentum-beta

Trend Continuation + Breakout Expansion flood the BUY list. Factor attribution: both have β≈+0.75 to MOM and ~zero alpha — they make money only from momentum exposure and bleed in chop. The genuine alpha (VCP / 52wk / 10WP, +11.5% on 10WP) lives in low-volume setups that rarely reach BUY.

② Regime mismatch: edge is trending-only, market is choppy

PF 1.59 trending vs 0.99 choppy. 79% of signals fire in the regime where the system has no edge. No regime-conditional throttle sizes this down.

③ entry_quality gate is inverted

It demotes EXTENDED/MISSED (the profitable breakout entries) to WATCH and keeps FRESH (PF 0.67, losing). ~410 of 462 breakout WATCH-demotions are EXTENDED/MISSED — the gate is actively removing edge.

④ Verdict line is mis-cut

BUY ≤ WATCH on every metric. The score→BUY threshold isn't where the edge separates. The scoring does rank (WATCH PF 1.26 proves discrimination exists) — the cut is just in the wrong place / on the wrong axes.

⑤ Edge erosion is unmanaged

April→June PF collapse went un-throttled. There's a rolling-Sharpe kill but it's currently OFF (disabled 06-07 after a false-trip emptied the universe).

6 · Recommendations (prioritized, no new data licenses)

P0 Fix the ranking layer — the score is anti-predictive at the top

§4 shows score (r=−0.10), catalyst tier, conviction tier and entry_quality all fail to rank outcomes. Don't keep adding pillars to a composite that inverts. Two moves: (a) regime-segment the score — it works in trending, fails in choppy, so calibrate/score separately per regime rather than one global number; (b) cap or quarantine the 90+ Breakout-in-choppy cohort that's poisoning the top band. Rebuild the conviction/catalyst tiers against realized PF, not a-priori labels.

P0 Re-cut the verdict on the axes that actually predict

Since BUY underperforms WATCH, the score threshold is the wrong gate. Gate BUY on the dimensions the data says carry edge: regime ∈ {trending, bull}, entry ∈ {PULLBACK}, setup ∈ {VCP, 52wk, 10WP, Trend-Cont}. The mechanism already exists — signal_filter.py (A2 whitelist, OFF by default). Build the whitelist from these audited cells and A/B it.

P1 Regime-conditional kill: Breakout Expansion + TC longs in choppy

Largest family is a net loser (PF 0.90), concentrated in choppy. The 2026-06-08 history audit already showed a regime-conditional kill lifts actionable PF to ~1.55 in-sample. Validate via walk-forward before applying (principle 1 — in-sample isn't enough).

P1 Ship the entry_quality fix for breakout-alpha setups

The bypass is staged (enabled 06-09, paper-only). The verdict-gated backtest that blocked validation is now fixed (commit 0b85d257e). Run it and promote if it clears the PF≥1.20 / 4-fold gate.

P1 Resolve a live config contradiction on VCP

VCP is one of the few setups with real alpha — yet universal_setup_multiplier (the live source when portfolio-blind) sets VCP Breakout = 0.0× (killed) while the entry_quality bypass simultaneously promotes VCP. These two layers disagree. Verify intent and reconcile — you may be killing your best setup.

P2 Verify PEAD / ESP / Insider data wiring

5 of 7 new sleeves have produced 0 signals ever. Three (Momentum/Defensive/MeanRev) are legitimately regime-dormant. But PEAD/ESP/Insider show None inputs universe-wide (ESP=None, days_since_earnings=None) — the same silent-input failure mode behind the 9 PEAD bugs (Audit Batch 2). Confirm the Zacks ESP, earnings-date, and insider feeds are actually populated.

P2 Add an edge-decay throttle

Size down (or halt) as rolling realized PF falls below the retail floor. A simple 30-trade rolling-PF monitor would have flagged the April→June collapse in mid-May.
