3 · The fix — regime-conditional rank_score (shipped + activated)

What shipped (RANK-REBUILD-2026-06-13)

  • signal_rank.py — rank from the pre-bonus pillar norms (bonus structurally excluded), catalyst+quality-led in choppy, momentum-inclusive in trending.
  • Composite demoted to a ≥60 quality gate; rank_score does the ranking + BUY promotion.
