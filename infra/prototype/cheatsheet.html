📋 Pre-Trade Cheat Sheet

Every check, in order, before you click Buy. Built for someone trading their first month — assume nothing, verify everything.

Author: SwingTrade engine Last updated: 2026-05-04 Print to keep beside your monitor

⚡ If you only have 60 seconds — the 5 commandments

  1. Regime first. Check the regime banner. Panic or Risk-Off Trending → no longs today, no exceptions.
  2. Score ≥ 70 AND stars ≥ 4. Anything below = WATCH only. The tail filter exists because below this, you bleed money.
  3. R:R ≥ 3:1. The trade plan must show 3.0+ reward-to-risk. Below 3 = skip, the math doesn't work over a year.
  4. Read the Plan tab. Why-BUY narrative, invalidation level, hold period. If you can't explain the trade in one sentence — don't take it.
  5. Stop loss BEFORE entry. Place a bracket order. Never enter without an automated stop. The stop is not optional.

9Position sizing

Open Positions tab · check current allocations · use Quick Buy with suggested size.

📐 Position-size formula:
shares = floor( (equity × risk_pct × regime_mult × vix_mult × dd_mult) / (entry - stop) )
Example: $100,000 × 1% × 1.0 × 0.9 × 1.0 = $900 risk · entry $50 stop $48 → 450 shares ($22,500 position = 22.5% allocation, 0.9% account risk).

📖Glossary — every acronym explained

Score
0-100 composite from 5 pillars: Tech 35 + Catalyst 20 + RS 20 + Smart Money 15 + Quality 10.
Stars
1-5 quality indicator. Stars < 4 triggers tail-loss filter — system auto-demotes BUY → WATCH.
R:R
Reward-to-risk ratio. (target1 - entry) / (entry - stop). Must be ≥ 3.0 for swing trades.
R-multiple
Trade outcome measured in units of risk. Stop hit = -1R. T1 hit = +1R. T2 hit = +2R+. Avg-R per trade is the true performance metric.
Conviction tier
T1 (full size, score 88+ R:R 3.5+) · T2 (50% size, score 78+) · T3 (25% size) · WATCH (0% — alert only).
Setup family
4 buckets: Impulse Catalyst (PEAD, UOA, gap-and-go) · Breakout Expansion (VCP, 52wk, squeeze) · Trend Continuation (EMA pullback, bounce, rebreak) · Special Situation (insider, float, squeeze).
Regime4
4-state market classification: risk_on_trending (best) · risk_on_choppy · risk_off_trending · panic (no longs).
VIX
CBOE Volatility Index. Implied vol of S&P options. <15 = complacent · 15-25 = normal · 25-35 = stressed · >35 = panic.
RS
Relative Strength vs SPY over 63 days, ranked 0-99. RS > 75 = leading · < 50 = lagging.
RVOL
Relative Volume — today's volume / 30-day average. RVOL > 1.5 = institutional interest. Lower = quiet day.
EMA
Exponential Moving Average. Bullish stack: price > EMA8 > EMA21 > EMA50.
RSI
Relative Strength Index (14-period). 30-70 = healthy. <30 = oversold (or falling knife). >70 = overbought.
MACD
Moving Average Convergence Divergence. Momentum oscillator. Bullish = above signal line + zero.
ATR
Average True Range — daily volatility in $. Stops typically = 1.25× ATR below entry. Targets = 3-4× ATR above.
Squeeze
Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channel = volatility compression. Squeeze fire = explosive move incoming.
VCP
Volatility Contraction Pattern (Mark Minervini). Tightening base ahead of breakout. Strong setup if RS > 80.
PEAD
Post-Earnings Announcement Drift. Stocks that beat continue to rise for 30-60 days post-report.
UOA
Unusual Options Activity — calls volume >> OI on big strikes. Often precedes news/earnings.
OI
Open Interest — total contracts outstanding for an option strike.
P/C ratio
Put/Call ratio. <0.7 = bullish skew · >1.2 = bearish skew. Use for confirmation.
IV rank
Implied Volatility percentile vs 1-year history. 0-100. Low IV = options cheap (calls/puts both).
Max pain
Strike where most options expire worthless near OPEX. Stocks often drift toward it on monthly expiry.
OPEX
Options Expiration. Monthly = 3rd Friday. Weekly = every Friday. Big-vol days, often pinning at max-pain.
Bracket order
Single order with entry + stop + take-profit. Stop and T1 are OCO (one cancels the other). MUST USE.
OCO
One-Cancels-Other. When one leg fills, the other auto-cancels. Used in brackets.
SMC
Smart Money Concepts — BOS (break of structure), CHoCH (change of character), order blocks, FVG (fair value gap).
BOS / CHoCH
Bullish BOS = trend continuing up. Bearish CHoCH = trend reversal possible.
OBV / CMF
On-Balance Volume / Chaikin Money Flow. Both measure smart money flow. Rising = accumulation.
HMM regime
Hidden Markov Model 3-state classifier. Soft probabilities for bull/neutral/bear regimes.
CVaR
Conditional Value-at-Risk — average loss in worst 2.5% of cases. -8% CVaR = bad days lose 8%+.
Basel zone
Traffic-light VaR exception monitor. GREEN (≤4 exceptions / 250 trades) = healthy · YELLOW = monitor · RED = recalibrate.
Kupiec POF
Proportion-of-Failures test. Verifies VaR exception count matches expected. p > 0.05 = model OK.
Christoffersen
Conditional coverage test. Checks exceptions are not clustered in time. p > 0.05 = independent.
Tail filter
System gate: score < 60 OR stars < 4 = auto-demote to WATCH. Drops -2R losses dramatically.
Drawdown
% decline from peak equity. >10% triggers position-size reduction. >15% = ×0.20 size (safety mode).
Kelly fraction
Optimal-growth bet size from win rate × R:R. We use 1/4 Kelly (safer) for actual sizing.
