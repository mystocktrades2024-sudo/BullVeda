Leveraged ETF Rules
Decay
3× leverage loses ~15-20%/yr to daily rebalancing in a sideways market. Never hold long-term.
Hold period
1 intraday to 2 days MAXIMUM. These are not swing vehicles.
Position size
Max 2% of portfolio. Treat as 6–9% notional exposure.
VIX limit
No leveraged ETF trades when VIX > 25. Volatility drag is lethal.
Trend requirement
Only trade leveraged ETFs with the trend direction. No counter-trend bets.
Entry timing
First 30 min of session can be whippy. Wait for direction to establish after 10 AM ET.

Crypto Rules for Equity Traders
Weekend gap risk
Crypto trades 24/7. BTC can gap 5–15% over the weekend. Stocks open Monday reflecting that move. Size down into Friday close.
Regime alignment
Only long crypto-correlated equities when BTC is above its 50-day MA. Below 50d = no longs.
Correlation
MSTR / COIN correlate ~0.8–0.9 to BTC. Miners correlate ~0.85–0.95 at extremes.
mNAV for MSTR
MSTR price / (BTC holdings/shares × BTC price). Above 1.5× = expensive. Below 0.8× = value.
