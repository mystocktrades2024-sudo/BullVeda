— How it works

A pipeline, not a watchlist.

Every morning at 06:00 PT, 800-1000 tickers go through a four-stage filter. By 08:00 PT, you have a ranked, regime-gated list of what (if anything) to trade today.

01 · Universe
Liquid US equities.
S&P 500 + Russell 1000 + custom watchlist, filtered to $5-$250 price, ≥$10M daily dollar volume, no ≤7d earnings.
02 · 5 Pillars
Score 0-100.

03 · Hard gates
Discipline floor.
Wilson-LB kill list · regime gate (no longs in panic) · R:R ≥ 3.0 · mechanism hypothesis required. One fail = out.
04 · Top 5 elite
Per mode × stage.

— What's inside

Six things hedge funds do that retail platforms don't.

Every layer is replayable, auditable, and reproducible. The system is its own witness.

⌾
Regime-aware sizing.
Four regimes — risk-on trending, risk-on choppy, risk-off trending, panic — each with its own BAP threshold and max position size. Misclassifying regime is worse than any other bug; the classifier is hardened with HMM + breadth + VIX cross-checks.
∼
Wilson 95% lower-bound.
Setups are killed only when their Wilson lower-bound win rate is below the band threshold — not on point estimates. A 4W-0L hot streak doesn't get credited like 80W-20L. Sample size is honored, not averaged away.
∴
Mechanism-required.
Every setup family carries a one-sentence hypothesis of why it has edge. Pullback to EMA21 = institutional re-add. VCP breakout = supply absorption. If we can't explain it, we don't trade it.
⧗
Walk-forward backtests.
250-day train, 50-day test, 4 folds. Not a fitted curve — out-of-sample only. With ATR/ADV-scaled slippage, survivorship-haircut (–3pp WR), and gap-fill realism. Paper PF 1.5 ≈ real PF 1.2; we discount automatically.
◈
Monte-Carlo P(T1).
Probability the price hits target T1 before the stop, simulated over the holding window from current price + ATR + drift. Combined with R:R, this is the only "edge" number that matters.
◐
Adversarial honesty log.
Every decision is logged. Every gate is replayable. Every miss is published. Drift alerts. Calibration bars. If the system stops working, you'll see it before your P&L does.

— Frequently asked

Questions we got before launch.

Is this a tipping service or a fund?+
Neither. Kairos is a research output — a daily ranked list of trades the system believes meet its statistical criteria, with full transparency on the gates each ticker passed or failed. You decide whether to trade any of them. We don't manage capital, take a cut, or front-run your orders. This is not investment advice.
What's the universe and the holding period?+
S&P 500 + Russell 1000 + a custom watchlist (≈1,000 tickers). The system surfaces 3 modes — Swing (2-10 day hold), Position (10-60 day hold), and Invest (60-180 day hold). Each is scored independently with its own gates.
Why "walk-forward" and not a single backtest?+
A single backtest is just a curve fitted to history. Walk-forward splits the timeline into rolling train + test windows and reports out-of-sample performance only — much closer to what you'd actually experience in live trading. Combined with the survivorship haircut (–3pp WR for today's index ≠ historical index) and ATR/ADV-scaled slippage, the numbers you see here are intentionally conservative.
What happens in a market crash?+
The regime classifier flips to risk-off-trending or panic automatically. In risk-off, the BAP threshold rises to 78 (very few names qualify) and max size drops to 35%. In panic (VIX > 35 or breadth < 20%), no new longs are surfaced at all. Existing positions have stop-tightening rules. This is why regime detection is the most important code in the system.
How are signals sized for live trading?+
Half-Kelly with three multipliers: regime (0.35-1.0), VIX (0.5-1.0), drawdown (0.5-1.0). The result is conservative by construction — capital preservation beats return optimization in the asymmetric world (losing 50% requires +100% to recover). Max 3-5 positions, 5-10% per position.
What about data quality?+
Primary data is EODHD (paid, institutional quality). Earnings ESP from a separate source. Options chain from Schwab Trader API. Insider transactions cross-checked against SEC EDGAR Form 4. Every scan reports 15 data-health checks; if more than 30% of tickers fail to fill, the scan aborts and we send a Slack alert.
Can I use this with my broker?+
Currently we surface the picks — execution is yours. We have an Alpaca paper-trading integration for testing; a live execution adapter is on the roadmap behind feature flags. Until you explicitly authorize live trading, no orders touch your account.
What's the cost?+
Free during the closed-beta window. Post-beta pricing TBD — likely a flat monthly subscription for the read-only research feed, with no AUM-linked or per-trade fees. We're explicitly avoiding any incentive structure that would tempt us to manufacture signal volume.
