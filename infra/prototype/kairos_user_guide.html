How to read this guide

Kairos is a rules-based swing-trading cockpit. It scans a universe of stocks every morning, scores each one, classifies the market regime, and routes every ticker to a verdict — BUY WATCH SHORT AVOID — with a full audit trail behind every call.

The live dashboard's left sidebar is grouped into four families. This guide follows the same order:

  • Main workspaces — your daily decision surfaces (Conviction Grid, Scanner, Portfolio, Performance…).
  • Markets — top-down context (regime, breadth, sectors, themes, crypto…).
  • Reference — the rulebook, glossary, settings, and system health.
  • Admin / Diagnostic — internal data-plane and research surfaces (mostly for the operator).
  • Per-ticker detail sub-tabs — the 14-tab drill-down that opens when you click any ticker.

Tips: click any card header to expand it · use the search box (top-left) to filter the whole document live · hit Print / PDF for a fully-expanded copy.
