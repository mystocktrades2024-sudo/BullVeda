Inside the terminal

Built by quants, for the way you actually trade

One scan. Five conviction setups.

Every morning Kairos ranks the universe by edge (R × Wilson LB), regime-fits it, and surfaces the handful worth your attention — each row enriched with RVOL, ML P(up), R:R, pivot distance and an earnings flag.

  • ✓ Regime & tape read with a multi-stage scan funnel
  • ✓ Composite bias reconciles every lens into one read
  • ✓ Best-ideas board: top 5 from each discovery engine

An ML edge you can actually audit.

A multi-head ensemble — direction, magnitude cone and hit-net — projects each name across swing / position / invest horizons, with calibration and feature importances shown openly. No black box.

  • ✓ Annotated price projection: MAs, breakout base, forward AI zone
  • ✓ Cross-mode disagreement flags trades vs investments
  • ✓ Calibration & feature importances shown — no black box

Options, priced like a desk.

A full options cockpit — chain, Greeks and vol surface — plus a Black-Scholes profit grid that maps P&L across price × time, with a manual calculator and six strategy presets from long calls to cash-secured puts.

  • ✓ Daily % return grid with spot line + target band
  • ✓ IV-shift & earnings-crush stress in one click
  • ✓ Buy/Write · Call/Put · live cost & credit

Risk at the book level, not just the ticker.

Most tools reason one name at a time. Kairos aggregates your whole book — factor tilts, gross/net/leverage, portfolio Greeks, concentration and per-name risk contribution — then suggests optimizer weights and hedges.

  • ✓ Barra-style factor exposure & net/gross/β
  • ✓ Optimizer + vol-targeting + return attribution
  • ✓ Crowding, capacity & hedge overlays
