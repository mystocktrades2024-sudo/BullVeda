Option · 03

Atelier Editorial

Push toward research / editorial. Warm cream surface, oxblood accent, Instrument Serif italic for ticker names + section titles, Source Serif for prose, JetBrains Mono only where numbers must align. Hairline dividers, square corners, generous whitespace. Reads like a printed research note from a boutique fund — less "terminal," more "the book Bridgewater PMs would buy." For the investor persona more than the day-trader.
