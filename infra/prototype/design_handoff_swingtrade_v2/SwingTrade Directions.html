Pitch

Each ticker you investigate gets pinned as a browser-style tab. Switch between names with ⌘1…9. Scan surfaces (Map, Watchlist, BUY) are themselves tabs. Closing a tab discards its lens state; pinning it survives reloads. Feels native to power-users who keep 5–8 names in working memory.

Why

  • Mirrors how an active operator already uses Chrome/IB
  • No slide-over panel — each ticker is its own room
  • Detail surface is always full-width

Tradeoffs

  • Cross-name compare is harder (no side-by-side)
  • Tab clutter risk if not auto-evicted

Pitch

Three co-equal columns: icon nav · ranked scan list · 14-lens detail. The middle column is always a sorted list (BUY / Watchlist / Map-results) and is the persistent "what next." Cmd-click any row to swap into the detail without losing your place. Most "Bloomberg-like" of the five.

Why

  • Three-pane is muscle memory for trading desks
  • Always one click from the next BUY candidate
  • Detail column gets ~65% width — generous

Tradeoffs

  • Market Map becomes a list view, not a treemap (or moves to a modal "Map" surface)
  • Rigid — less flexible than B for split work
